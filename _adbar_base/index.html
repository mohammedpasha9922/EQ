          Help & About

          Learn how to use EQ and discover its features.

          About the App

          EQ is a smart, all-in-one calculator that combines everyday math, scientific and percentage tools, currency conversion and much more in one simple, easy-to-use app.

          Why was EQ created?

          The idea is simple: one calculator instead of many, built for speed, clarity and everyday use.

          • •Fast everyday calculations
          • •Scientific tools such as square root, powers and parentheses
          • •Easy percentage calculations
          • •Currency conversion and live rates
          • •Notes and calculation history
          • •Simple, clear and quick to use
          • •Works as an installable app (PWA) on different devices

          Explaining the App Sections

          General Calculator

          The main calculator for everyday operations: add, subtract, multiply and divide.

          Example: 12 + 7 = 19.

          Scientific Tools

          Tap "Scientific" to use the square root, square and parentheses buttons inside the same calculator.

          Example: √9 = 3, 2^3 = 8.

          Percentage Calculator

          Quickly work out a percentage of an amount without extra steps.

          Example: 15% of 200 = 30.

          History

          EQ remembers what you calculated in the last 24 hours so you can review or share it.

          Notes

          Save quick notes, organize them in folders, and edit them in a full-screen editor.

          Currency Tools

          Search currencies, see live rates, convert between currencies, use a custom rate, and keep your favorites.

          Settings

          Change the language, the theme and the sound feedback exactly how you like.

          How to Use the Calculator

          0–9Tap to type digits.
          +Adds the next number.
          −Subtracts the next number.
          ×Multiplies by the next number.
          ÷Divides by the next number.
          =Shows the result.
          ACClears everything and starts fresh.
          ⌫Deletes the last digit you typed.
          .Adds a decimal point.
          SciScientific / Percentage: switches the extra tools on and off.
          🔊Speaks the current result aloud.

          Settings

          Language: switches the whole app between the available languages.

          Theme: choose Dark, Light or Violet appearance.

          App Sounds: the master switch for sound and haptic feedback.

          When App Sounds is ON, button sound and haptics are allowed. Turn it OFF to silence that feedback, and ON again to allow it.

          Speech/TTS is separate from App Sounds and is not turned off by App Sounds.

          Currency Converter

          Pick the currency you have (From) and the one you want (To), then type an amount.

          Use the swap button to reverse the two currencies.

          Use the star to mark a currency as a favorite, and open Favorite Currencies from the currency menu.

          Convert at Custom Rate lets you enter your own exchange rate.

          Live prices come from the online service; if it is unavailable, cached rates may be used.

          Install & Offline

          You can install EQ as an app on supported devices.

          Some features work offline using stored resources, but live currency rates and app updates need an internet connection.
